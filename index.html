The problem

Review scores rarely capture how a film actually feels to talk about with friends — the running jokes, the plot holes, the "why didn't they just call someone" moments.

The approach

CineMe pairs each film's real reputation with a randomly generated, deliberately unserious counter-review — a lightweight way to explore a multilingual catalogue through humor instead of star ratings alone.

What's under the hood

A single-file client-side app: no backend, no build step. Favorites persist locally, filtering runs client-side across genre and language, and results are shareable with one click.
